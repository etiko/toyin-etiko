## The opportunity

CakesbyBIMS' previous website ran on an ageing WordPress theme carrying stock
photography and Lorem Ipsum copy on its About and Contact pages, despite the
business having genuine cake photography and a loyal following. The brief I
set myself was to keep everything that already worked — the "Baked with Love"
story and real social links — while replacing the rest with a faster, more
personal experience.

## The approach

I rebuilt the site with Next.js App Router, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS v4,
giving each navigation item its own dedicated route rather than mixing
scroll-anchors into the nav. Real product photography from the business's
media library replaced stock imagery throughout, and a filterable gallery
with a lightbox gives visitors a proper way to browse past work by occasion.

Service pricing was deliberately removed: the previous site never published
real prices, so every service now links to a contact page built around a
tailored quote instead of a fabricated "from £X" figure. The enquiry form
itself needed no backend — it opens the visitor's email client with a
pre-filled message, keeping the project dependency-free to host and maintain.

<ProjectNote title="Keeping what already worked">
	Not every legacy detail needed replacing. Genuine copy, real photography and
	existing social proof were preserved and rebuilt around, rather than
	discarded for the sake of a clean slate.
</ProjectNote>
