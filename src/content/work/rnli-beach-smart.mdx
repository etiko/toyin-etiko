## The opportunity

The RNLI wanted to help children understand beach safety through an experience
that felt playful and memorable rather than instructional.

The result was a three-part game built around short challenges and familiar
beach scenarios.

## The experience

Players help characters swim between the safety flags, position the correct
flags on the beach, and identify children who need to buddy up before entering
the water. Each interaction turns a safety principle into something children
can actively practise.

<ProjectNote title="Designing for the audience">
	The controls and feedback needed to be immediately understandable, allowing
	the safety message to remain central to the experience.
</ProjectNote>

## Contribution

- Developed the front-end interactions for all three game stages.
- Translated safety guidance into clear, age-appropriate tasks.
- Created mouse-driven controls and responsive visual feedback.
- Worked within a wider campaign delivered by Proximity London.
