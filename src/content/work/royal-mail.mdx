## The opportunity

Royal Mail's public website needed reusable interface components that could
support a broad set of services and remain dependable across its supported
devices and browsers.

I worked as part of the Proximity London front-end team, collaborating with
Capgemini, who delivered the Drupal-powered back end.

## The approach

Rather than treating each page as an isolated build, the team created modular
components from the ground up. This gave us greater control over behaviour,
testing, and consistency across the website.

Accessibility was integral to delivery. Components went through rigorous
testing to meet DDA expectations and provide a comparable experience across
supported platforms.
